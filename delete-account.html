Delete Your Prep Thamizha Account

Last updated: 11 October 2026

This page explains how to delete your Prep Thamizha account and the data linked to it.

How to delete your account (in the app)

  1. Open Prep Thamizha and sign in.
  2. Go to the Me tab.
  3. In the Danger Zone, tap Delete Account and confirm.

Your account is paused for 2 days. If you change your mind, sign in again during this time and your account is restored. After 2 days it is deleted automatically, usually within 3 days of your request.

No access to the app?

Email prepthamizha@gmail.com from the email address you used to sign in, with the subject "Delete my account". We will process it within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile (display name, device ID, selected exam)
  • Your practice activity, bookmarks and streaks
  • Any feedback you sent, including your email and message
  • Your sign-in account (name, email and user ID in Firebase)

What is kept

  • Purchase records (purchase token, product, plan, amount, exam and dates) with your user ID removed, for as long as accounting and tax rules require
  • Google Play keeps its own billing records under Google's policies
  • Our hosting provider may keep server logs for a limited time

If you never bought a plan, no purchase record exists.

Your subscription

Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it first in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions.

Delete only your practice data

Use Reset Progress in the Me tab. It deletes your attempts, bookmarks and streaks, and keeps your account.
